# 0.1.0 — sound voronoi

## goal

A canvas experiment: the Voronoi diagram of mass points is the terrain, a probe
flies through it under the points' gravity, and the cell under the probe plus its
two nearest neighbours are heard as a small synth. Everything runs from
`index.html` with classic scripts (file:// safe, no build, no network).

## canvas

- Full-viewport canvas, DPR aware, one `requestAnimationFrame` loop.
- A point is a Voronoi site. Radius 7..20 px is drawn and sets the mass
  `m = (r/12)²`, so a big point owns a bigger cell and pulls harder.
- The diagram is built by brute-force half-plane clipping of the view rect per
  site (`n <= 400`), not by Delaunay:
  - the clip predicate is the affine form `f(p) = |p-si|² - |p-sj|² = a·x + b·y + c`,
    which is exact along an edge with one multiply-add per test;
  - a site is skipped when `d² > 4·reach²` (`reach` = farthest current vertex from
    the site): then every vertex is already closer to i than to j;
  - each cell keeps a flat polygon (stride 64), its shoelace area and its reach;
  - adjacency reuses the predicate: j neighbours i iff a vertex of cell i lies on
    the bisector of i,j (`|f| <= 1e-4·d²`), restricted to `d² <= 4·reach²`, at most
    16 neighbours per cell.
- Rebuilds happen on add / move / remove / resize only. Per frame nothing is
  allocated: point buffers, polygons and the audio frame are preallocated and
  mutated in place.

## tones

- Pitch grid: pentatonic major degrees over two octaves above C3 (130.81 Hz).
- Zone key: every 260x220 px zone takes a root from the circle of fifths
  (`0, 7, 2, 9, 5`). Neighbouring zones share four of five pitch classes, so
  borders stay consonant, while the same degree is not repeated canvas-wide.
- A new point scores its ten candidate degrees against the points within 340 px
  (interval dissonance table, nearer points weigh more, unison is expensive) and
  takes the cheapest; the position hash only breaks ties, keeping placement
  deterministic.
- Measured on random 26-point fields: ~8 % of adjacent pairs share a tone, < 1 %
  are a minor second or a tritone, most are fifths, fourths and thirds.
- Colour = hue of the semitone on the chromatic wheel (equal pitches share a
  colour). The sounding cells are outlined, the one under the probe brightest.

## probe

- Drag on empty canvas = slingshot: the probe is placed at the press point while
  aiming (simulation frozen), released with the drag vector (gain 2.4, clamped to
  980 px/s).
- Gravity `a = G·m·(si - p) / ((r² + soft)·r)` with `soft = 60²`, mild drag, walls
  bounce with restitution 0.78. A probe slower than 14 px/s for 2.5 s is nudged in
  a random direction, so the piece never silences itself.
- Trail of the last 96 positions, pushed every 6 px.

## sound

Three voices = the nearest cell + its two nearest adjacent cells. Graph per voice:
`osc → lowpass → tremolo gain → level → stereo pan → master → limiter`.

- mode **pitch / cell** (pad): pitch is the cell's tone (the nearest neighbour an
  octave up, the second one an octave down); volume falls with the distance to the
  cell's point, neighbours scaled by their relative distance; 4.5 Hz shimmer.
  The slider sets the distance → volume falloff.
- mode **tone / distance**: for each of the three cells, pitch follows
  `55·2^(u·span/12)` with `u = d / (0.55·diagonal)` and the tremolo rate
  `0.1·300^(1-u)`, i.e. 30 Hz near … 0.1 Hz far, which becomes an AM timbre up
  close. The slider sets the pitch span.
- `pan = ±0.9` from the horizontal direction to the cell.
- All parameters use `setTargetAtTime`, so no JS smoothing state is needed and a
  lost frame never clicks.
- Audio starts on the first gesture; `M` mutes, `P` pauses (suspend).

## controls

click empty: add point · drag empty: launch probe · drag a point: move ·
right-click a point: remove · R randomize · C clear · 1/2 sound mode ·
M mute · P pause · space nudges the probe
