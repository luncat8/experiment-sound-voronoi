# 0.1.0 — worklog

Implemented `archive/draft.md` (voronoi field + gravity probe + two synth modes).

## decisions

- **Clipping, not Delaunay.** With n <= 400 sites, clipping each cell against the
  view rect is O(n²) but branch-light, has no degenerate geometry to special-case,
  and yields exact cell polygons — which the adjacency test and the rendering both
  need for free. Adjacency falls out of the same affine predicate, so there is no
  second structure to keep in sync. 300 sites rebuild in ~1.7 ms, i.e. one frame
  budget even while dragging a point.
- **Tone placement is a placement problem, not a tuning table.** A fixed pentatonic
  ladder would make adjacent cells dissonant whenever the ladder jumps; instead a
  new point scores ten candidate degrees against its neighbours with a dissonance
  table, and a circle-of-fifths zone root keeps the same degree from repeating
  canvas-wide. The two constraints conflict (avoiding repeats pushes towards
  dissonance), so the unison penalty and the hash jitter were tuned against
  measured data rather than by ear: unison went 34 % → 8 % while harsh intervals
  stayed under 1 %.
- **One voice graph, three voices, both modes.** The draft's two modes differ only
  in what drives pitch/amplitude, so a single node graph with `setTargetAtTime`
  covers both; a mode switch retunes the oscillators instead of rebuilding nodes.
  No JS smoothing state means no clicks when a frame is late.
- **Distance ranges are calibrated to the field, not to the canvas.** The nearest
  point is rarely farther than a third of the diagonal, so the tremolo's 0.1..30 Hz
  span and the pitch span are mapped over `0.55 × soundRef`; mapping them over the
  whole canvas made the extremes unreachable in practice.
- **Stall recovery instead of an airtight force model.** In a strong well the probe
  can settle; a nudge after 2.5 s of near-zero speed keeps the piece alive without
  a visible reset.

## steps and validation

1. `src/voronoi.js` + `experiments/check-voronoi.js` — areas tile the view to 1e-6
   relative, cells convex and inside the view, every vertex nearest to its own
   site, random probes land in `nearest()`'s cell, adjacency symmetric, collinear
   and single-site degenerate cases, 300 sites 1.7 ms.
2. `src/physics.js` + `experiments/check-physics.js` — 60 s flight stays finite, in
   bounds, under the speed clamp, travels 20 k px with 0.1 % idle frames, stall
   nudge works in a single heavy well, ballistic bounces in an empty field.
3. `src/tones.js` + `experiments/check-tones.js` — measured interval statistics over
   40 random fields (8.3 % unison, 0.7 % harsh, 27 % thirds, everything on the
   pentatonic grid), register stays C3..B4, distance mappings hit their exact
   endpoints and are monotonic.
4. `src/sound.js`, `src/app.js`, `index.html` + `experiments/smoke.js` — the real
   page scripts run against a stub DOM/Web Audio: 120+ frames, every gesture
   (add, ignore-too-close, slingshot launch, drag a point, right-click remove),
   keys, radio buttons, slider, resize and teardown; mode 1 pitches are cell tones
   an octave apart, mode 2 pitches leave the tuning and follow the probe, tremolo
   stays within 0.05..30 Hz and sweeps its range in flight, all voices mute when
   the field is cleared.
5. Visual pass: the scene was rasterised offline (software renderer, same maths)
   to compare cell-brightness candidates; the readable middle was kept, and the
   three sounding cells got their own outline weights.

`npm test` runs all four suites.

## known limits

- Cell polygons cap at 64 vertices and 16 neighbours per cell; both are far above
  what a 400-site field produces, but they are silent caps.
- No touch equivalent of right-click removal; touch users can use `C`/`R`.
- Mode 1's timbre is three sines; a wavetable or a detuned pair would sound
  fuller, at the cost of more nodes.
