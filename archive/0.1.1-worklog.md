# 0.1.1 — worklog (sound fix round)

Report after 0.1.0: *visual good, physics good, sound wrong — no proper frequency
characteristic, no polyphony, no LR direction, and the envelope mode does not
read as "rare far / often close"*.

## root causes

1. **No polyphony, wrong pitch.** The two neighbour voices were played an octave
   up and an octave down (`×2`, `×0.5`). Octave doublings fuse with the root into
   one perceived pitch, so three voices sounded like one, and the neighbours did
   not sound like their own cell — the colour → pitch link was broken.
2. **No LR direction.** Pan was proportional to distance:
   `pan = dx · 0.9 / (width/2)`. Inside a cell `|dx|` is ~50..150 px, so pan sat
   at ±0.1..0.2 — below the audible threshold. Direction has to be normalised by
   distance, not scaled by it.
3. **Envelope mode read as a wobble.** A sine tremolo of a steady tone is a smooth
   amplitude wobble at any rate; it never articulates as discrete events. On top
   of that, `u` was normalised by the canvas diagonal, and the nearest point is
   almost never a third of that away, so the whole 0.1..30 Hz span was compressed
   into 2..15 Hz and the extremes were unreachable.

## changes

- `src/sound.js`: each voice is a triangle carrier → lowpass → envelope gain →
  level → pan. The envelope LFO goes through a WaveShaper: sine + identity curve
  in the sustained mode, sawtooth + `attack(2%)·exp(-4.2·phase)` in the pulsed
  mode, so one cycle is one pluck. Mode switching swaps the curve and drops the
  modulation gain before moving the envelope base (otherwise base + depth add up
  to a doubled voice for ~80 ms).
- `src/app.js`: voices keep their own cell tone (1.0 / 0.85 / 0.7 with a
  proximity weight clamped to 0.45..1); pan is `0.92·dx/|d|` faded over the first
  70 px; distances are normalised per cell (`cellRadius` from the cell
  circumradius, clamped 70..420 px) for both the level curve and the pulse rate;
  the sustained level curve became `0.5/(1+u²(1+4·slider))`, so volume now
  actually changes inside a cell.
- `src/tones.js`: `tremolo()` renamed to `pulseRate()` (it is an articulation
  rate, not a tremolo depth), plus `pitch()`.
- Tests: `experiments/stub-page.js` now holds the stub DOM + recording Web Audio
  (shared by the checks and the measurements) and exposes `page.voice(i)` so tests
  read the real params instead of guessing node order. New checks: all carriers on
  cell tones, distinct pitches per frame, pans on both sides over a flight, pulse
  LFO is a sawtooth in the pulsed mode, envelope curve starts at silence, peaks at
  the start, decays monotonically, stays in 0..1.
- New measurement tools: `measure-sound.js` (parameters over a 45 s flight) and
  `render-audio.js` (offline synth render to WAV + peak/RMS/clipping/attack-rate
  analysis).

## measurements

`experiments/logs/0.1.1-sound.txt`, 45 s flights, 26 sites:

- pitch/cell: 3.0 voices and 3.0 distinct pitches per frame (was 1 perceived
  pitch), pan mean |pan| 0.57 with 43 % left / 45 % right samples, all pitches on
  the cell grid, level per voice 0.26 and now varying with `u`.
- tone/distance: pulse rate 0.10 .. 25.4 Hz with 35 % of samples below 0.5 Hz and
  11 % above 8 Hz across two flights — the full span is reachable and both
  extremes actually occur. Pitch 57..251 Hz, 193 distinct values.
- Offline render (20 s, `sound-distance.wav`): peak 0.61, no clipped samples,
  RMS 0.10, ~5 distinct envelope attacks per second, no silent windows.

## validation

`npm test` — voronoi, physics, tones and the page smoke suite, all green
(repeated runs; the only stochastic assertion is that pans reach both sides and
the pulse rate sweeps a wide range, both sampled over a flight).

## known limits

- Three voices only, per the draft; a fourth "bass anchor" would thicken the
  chord but moves away from "nearest cell + adjacent cells".
- The pulsed mode at its fastest (30 Hz) is an AM timbre by design; close to a
  point it is deliberately buzzy.
- The offline renderer approximates the Web Audio graph (no compression, one-pole
  filter), so it is for auditioning and signal checks, not for exact level
  comparison.
