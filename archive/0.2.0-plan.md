# 0.2.0 — modular voronoi synth

## goal

Orthogonal spatial synthesis over a Voronoi gravity field: a flying probe excites
only its containing Voronoi cell and that cell's Voronoi-adjacent cells, while
three independent radio groups control frequency source, volume law, and sine
envelope modulation, each paired with its own distance-steepness slider. Runs
directly from `index.html` over `file://` with classic `<script>` tags.

## field and pentatonic coloring

- Points (`n <= 400`) define Voronoi cells via half-plane polygon clipping of the
  viewport rectangle (`src/voronoi.js`), producing exact convex polygons, areas,
  circumradius reach, and symmetric Voronoi neighbor lists.
- Spatial pentatonic key zones (`260x220` px) select roots from the circle of
  fifths (`0, 7, 2, 9, 5`) over two octaves (`C3..B4`, semitones `0..23`).
  Adjacent zones share four of five pitch classes while shifting scale content
  across the canvas so the same five tones do not repeat globally.
- After Voronoi adjacency is built, two coordinate-descent passes (`tuneField`)
  score each site's ten local pentatonic candidates directly against its actual
  Voronoi neighbors (penalising unison and semitone/tritone clashes), eliminating
  same-note neighbors and harsh intervals across cell borders.
- Cell hue encodes pitch class (`0..330` deg) and lightness separates the two
  octaves so every cell tone is visually legible.

## active neighborhood and voice pinning

- Only the nearest site `near` (`voronoi.nearest(px, py)`) and its exact Voronoi
  neighbors (`voronoi.nbr` of `near`) generate sound; all non-adjacent cells stay
  silent.
- `Sound` maintains a preallocated pool of 20 Web Audio voices (`triangle` ->
  `lowpass` -> `envGain` -> `levelGain` -> `stereoPanner` -> `master` ->
  `compressor`, with a `sine` LFO -> `modGain` -> `envGain.gain`).
- Voices are pinned by `cellId` across frames:
  - cells remaining in `{near} U adj(near)` keep their assigned voice slot,
    preserving oscillator/LFO phase and avoiding false pitch glides or pan jumps;
  - newly entering cells claim an idle slot with frequency, pan, and LFO state
    snapped at gain zero before ramping level up;
  - cells leaving `{near} U adj(near)` ramp smoothly to zero before releasing
    their slot.

## three synthesis axes and steepness curves

Each distance curve takes Euclidean distance `d` (px) from the probe to the
voice's site and a steepness slider `s` in `0..1` mapping geometrically to a
spatial decay scale `scale = maxPx * (minPx / maxPx)^s`:

1. **Frequency** (`freq-point` | `freq-dist`, slider `freq-steep`):
   - `point id`: `130.81 * 2^(semitone / 12)` Hz.
   - `distance`: `10 * 1000^exp(-d / scale)` Hz (`scale` in `280..20` px), hitting
     `10 kHz` at `d = 0` and decaying smoothly to `10 Hz` at far distances.
     Lowpass cutoff tracks `clamp(freq * 4.5, 90, 18000)` Hz so `10 kHz` passes
     unattenuated while `10 Hz` retains gentle low triangle harmonics.
2. **Volume** (`vol-dist` | `vol-const`, slider `vol-steep`):
   - `distance`: `0.46 / (1 + (d / scale)^2)` (`scale` in `320..22` px).
   - `const`: equal-power constant gain `0.42 / sqrt(activeCount)` across all
     sounding cells in `{near} U adj(near)`, independent of distance.
3. **Volume envelope modulation** (`env-dist` | `env-off`, slider `env-steep`):
   - `off`: `depth = 0`, continuous gain `1`.
   - `distance`: pure `sine` modulation of `envGain`:
     - `d < 20` px: `depth = 0` (strictly continuous).
     - `20..28` px: smooth Hermite transition `depth: 0 -> 1`.
     - `d >= 20` px: rate `0.1 * 300^exp(-(d - 20) / scale)` Hz (`scale` in
       `300..28` px), sweeping from `30 Hz` in the mid range down to `0.1 Hz`
       far away.
     - Unipolar sine law: `envGain = 1 - 0.5 * depth`, `modGain = 0.5 * depth`
       with identical smoothing constants so `envGain + modGain = 1` at all times.

## stereo direction

- Each voice pans by the horizontal unit vector from the probe to its point:
  `pan = clamp(0.95 * (dx / dist) * min(1, dist / 20), -1, 1)`. Points to the
  left/right stay clearly panned inside the cell, fading gently through center
  within the `20` px core.
