# 0.2.0 — worklog (modular radio-group synth + full Voronoi neighborhood)

## review of 0.1.1

Reviewed `0.1.0` / `0.1.1` before implementing `0.2.0` (`archive/0.2.0-draft.md`)
and identified five architectural and behavioural issues:

1. **Incomplete neighborhood & voice-swap glides.** `0.1.1` capped voices at `3`
   (`near` + 2 closest neighbors), dropping 3–6 other Voronoi-adjacent cells and
   causing `voice[1]`/`voice[2]` to glide in pitch and pan across the stereo field
   whenever two neighbors swapped distance rank.
2. **Coupled 2-mode design & inverted frequency curve.** `0.1.1` coupled pitch,
   volume, and envelope into 2 radio buttons, mapped distance pitch *upward*
   (`55..250 Hz` instead of `10 kHz` at `0 px` down to `10 Hz` far), and capped
   the lowpass filter at `6–9 kHz` (which would attenuate `10 kHz`).
3. **Sawtooth WaveShaper instead of `<20 px` continuous + `30..0.1 Hz` sine.**
   `0.1.1` gated voices with a sawtooth pluck curve and clamped `u = d / cellRef`
   at `1`, which pinned almost all adjacent cells flat at `0.1 Hz` and had no
   `<20 px` continuous core.
4. **Sequential Euclidean tone picking left 8.3 % unison neighbors.** Points
   inserted earlier were never re-evaluated against points inserted right beside
   them later.
5. **Initial probe position.** `init()` never placed the probe, leaving it at
   `(0, 0)` on startup, and lacked an `N` point-count input.

## changes implemented

- **`src/tones.js`**:
  - Added `tuneCell` and 2-pass `tuneField` directly over `voronoi.nbr` after
    diagram construction, plus octave-distinct `lightness(semitone)` (`24` distinct
    cell colors across `C3..B4`).
  - Replaced coupled curves with orthogonal steepness-controlled functions:
    `distFreq(d, steep)` (`10 kHz` at `0 px` -> `10 Hz` far),
    `distVolume(d, steep)` (Lorentzian inverse-square falloff),
    `envDepth(d)` (`0` strictly inside `<20 px`, Hermite ramp `20..28 px` to `1`),
    and `envRate(d, steep)` (`30 Hz` at `20 px` mid-range onset -> `0.1 Hz` far).
- **`src/sound.js`**:
  - Expanded to a preallocated 20-voice pool (`Sound.MAX_VOICES = 20`) pinned by
    `cellId` (`slotCell`), snapping newly entering cells at zero gain before
    fading in and fading out exiting cells before slot reuse.
  - Pure `sine` LFO with unipolar gain law `envGain = 1 - 0.5*depth`,
    `modGain = 0.5*depth` sharing identical `setTargetAtTime` smoothing (`0.045 s`)
    so `envGain + modGain === 1` holds continuously without clicks.
  - Raised lowpass ceiling to `18 kHz` (`clamp(freq * 4.5, 90, 18000)`).
- **`index.html` + `src/app.js`**:
  - Replaced single mode toggle + single slider with 3 independent radio groups
    (`freq: point id | distance`, `volume: distance | const`,
    `envelope: distance | off`), 3 paired `steep` sliders (dimmed when inactive),
    and `N` points input.
  - `buildFrame()` excites `{near} U adj(near)` exclusively (`~5.8–6.5` voices on
    average, `0` non-adjacent voices), pans by LR unit direction (`PAN_REACH = 20`),
    draws subtle `20 px` core rings on sounding points when distance envelope is
    active, and places the probe at viewport center on startup.
- **`experiments/`**:
  - Updated `stub-page.js`, `check-tones.js`, `smoke.js`, `measure-sound.js`, and
    `render-audio.js` for the 20-voice pool and 3 radio groups.

    `pitch range 10.1..9335.0 Hz` (`787` distinct), equal constant voice gain,
    `env rate 0.10..30.00 Hz`.

  - `dist-const-env`: `peak 0.434`, `0` clipped samples, `RMS 0.0915`, `0 %` dead windows.
